User Data Deletion Instructions

This application is a university project used for educational purposes.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you would like your data to be deleted from our system, you may request deletion by following these steps:

  1. Send an email to: loperatomy@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request"
  3. Include the email address associated with your account.

What Happens Next

  • Your account and associated data will be removed from the system.
  • The deletion process may take up to 48 hours.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Authentication data stored in Amazon Web Services (AWS Cognito)
  • Basic profile information collected during login
